# The Addition of Two Integers.

In order to add two integers, you need two values, x and y.

For this example, let x and y be positive.

The formula to add integers is:

    x + y = z

where z is the sum of x and y.

In examples where x or y is NOT positive, take the absolute value of each and subtract.

The formula to do so is:

    |x| - |y| = z

where z is the difference of |x| and |y|.

In the case where x and y are NOT positive, add the absolute value of x and y and multiply z by -1.

The formula is:

    |x| + |y| = -1 * z

OR

    -1(|x| + |y|) = z

where z is the sum of |x| and |y|, and negative.

# Examples
Where x = 1; y = 3, find z.

z = x + y

z = 1 + 3

z = 4
